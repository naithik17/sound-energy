10.4

How does sound transfer energy?

When a sound source vibrates, it passes energy to the medium around it.

Particles are always moving randomly (thermal energy).

The sound makes them vibrate more. They bump into neighbours and pass the energy on.

They go back towards their usual random motion.

Sound isn't just a waveform...

A sound wave changes the density of the medium as it travels.

C = Compression (particles close together)   R = Rarefaction (particles far apart)

10.5

Same wave. Two ways of showing it!

Compression → higher density → graph rises  |  Rarefaction → lower density → graph falls

10.5

Crest & Trough

CREST: max density
TROUGH: min density

Does the air move up and down?
No! The particles move left and right. The graph's height shows density, not position.
